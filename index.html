Brant Family Health Records

A private, personal health record used only by the Brant family. It is not offered to the public.

With each family member's permission, it imports that person's own records from their healthcare providers (through the providers' standard patient-access APIs, signing in on the provider's own page) and shows them on a dashboard that runs only on the family's own computer: immunization histories, lab results over time, growth charts, prescriptions and documents, each linked back to its source.

How data is handled

  • Access is read-only, and only to records the signed-in person (or a child's parent) approves.
  • All records are stored on the family's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to any other server.
  • No data is shared, sold, or used for any purpose other than the family viewing its own records.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the provider's patient portal (for example MyChart) or from the app.
