Terms of use

Brant Family Health Records is a private app for the Brant family's personal use only. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else is permitted to use it.

It displays copies of records from your healthcare providers for your own convenience. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice; your providers' records remain the authoritative source.

By connecting a provider, you authorize the app to read the records you approve and store them on your own computer, as described in the privacy policy. You can revoke that access at any time.
